# Disclaimer

`ph-compliance-mcp` (Philippine Legal & Compliance MCP) is an **information
retrieval tool**, not a lawyer, not legal advice, and not a source of legal
counsel. Nothing this tool returns constitutes legal advice.

- Every tool response is citation-grounded or refused — never a best-effort guess.
- Compliance answers (payroll, accounting, HR, tax, governance) are retrieval
  of primary sources, not professional advice for a specific filing or audit.
- Laws change, corpora lag reality, and retrieval can miss or mis-rank. Verify
  anything you rely on against the official source.
- Use at your own risk.
