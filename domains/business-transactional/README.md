# Business & Transactional Law

**Owner:** `src/domains/business-transactional.ts` · **Corpus:** laws, cases, issuances · **Refresh:** medium

## Scope

Contracts, sales, agency, credit transactions, negotiable instruments (Act
2031), Ease of Doing Business (RA 11032), and consumer protection (RA 7394).

## Key instruments

- Civil Code obligations and contracts (RA 386)
- Negotiable Instruments Law (Act 2031)
- Ease of Doing Business Act (RA 11032)
- Consumer Act of the Philippines (RA 7394)
