# Human Resources & Workplace Compliance

**Owner:** `src/domains/human-resources.ts` · **Corpus:** laws, cases, issuances · **Refresh:** high

## Scope

DOLE issuances (Department Orders, Labor Advisories), labor standards,
termination rules, Occupational Safety and Health (RA 11058), service
incentive leave, employment contracts, and civil-service rules.

## Key instruments

- Labor Code of the Philippines (PD 442)
- Occupational Safety and Health Standards Law (RA 11058)
- Service Incentive Leave provisions (Labor Code)
- DOLE Department Orders and Labor Advisories
