# Payroll & Benefits Law

**Owner:** `src/domains/payroll.ts` · **Corpus:** laws, issuances · **Refresh:** high

## Scope

13th month pay (PD 851), SSS contributions (RA 11199), PhilHealth (RA 11223),
Pag-IBIG Fund (RA 9679), withholding tax on compensation, and related
contribution/remittance rules.

## Key instruments

- 13th Month Pay Law (PD 851)
- Social Security Act of 2018 (RA 11199)
- Universal Health Care Act PhilHealth provisions (RA 11223)
- Home Development Mutual Fund Law (RA 9679)
