# Accounting & Auditing Law

**Owner:** `src/domains/accounting.ts` · **Corpus:** laws, issuances · **Refresh:** medium

## Scope

Accountancy Act (RA 9298), Philippine Financial Reporting Standards (PFRS),
SEC and Board of Accountancy regulations, Commission on Audit rules on audit
of government entities and GOCCs.

## Key instruments

- Philippine Accountancy Act of 2004 (RA 9298)
- Revised Corporation Code audit provisions (RA 11232)
- COA rules and regulations on audit
